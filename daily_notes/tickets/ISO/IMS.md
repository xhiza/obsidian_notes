Exasol Security Incident Response Team (ExaSIRT)
	

    Responsible for addressing and handling all security events and incidents that are logged and identified by the organization's ITM department and employees

    has primary authority in response decisions for IT security incidents

    coordinates incidents from discovery through resolution and closure

    Assesses threats to IT resources

    categorizes and classifies incidents

    processes Information security complaints or incidents reported by other parties

    alerts employees of active threats

    prepares preliminary and final reports

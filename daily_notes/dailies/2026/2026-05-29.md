saas-staging management-node outage / postmortem
You created a postmortem for the saas-staging management node incident. The issue was a complete loss of management access to the staging management node, with no customer-facing impact. CPU was around 95%, restarts did not help, and SSM start-session was unavailable. Root cause was a broken resolv.conf symlink under /run/systemd/, which broke DNS resolution and prevented the SSM agent from reaching AWS endpoints. The recovery path was to create an EC2 Instance Connect Endpoint, SSH into the node, recreate/relink resolv.conf, restart systemd-resolved, verify DNS resolution, restart the SSM agent, and confirm SSM access worked again.  ￼

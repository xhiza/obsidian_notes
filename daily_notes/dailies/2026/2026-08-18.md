## Notes
- Account-name recovery: [exasol/saas-api-services#861](https://github.com/exasol/saas-api-services/pull/861) implements a public `POST /api/v1/register/recover-account-name` endpoint. It keeps the external response generic to avoid account enumeration, looks up org IDs by email, resolves Auth0 organization names, and sends the account-name list by SES.
- SES/Terraform support: [exasol/saas-terraform#1257](https://github.com/exasol/saas-terraform/pull/1257) computes the from-address per environment, publishes `SES_FROM_ADDRESS` and `SES_FROM_DOMAIN`, and intentionally uses the already-existing SES domain identities rather than creating/importing new identities.
- Email sender decision: discussed whether account-name recovery mail should come from `service@exasol.com` or `no-reply@cloud.exasol.com`; the leaning was `no-reply@cloud.exasol.com` because the mail is informational and does not invite a support reply. Auth0 was checked and treated as unsuitable for arbitrary account-name recovery content because its emails are tied to built-in Auth0 actions/templates.
- Release deploy investigation: the staging deploy exposed multiple go-utils-related failure modes. [exasol/saas-terraform#1255](https://github.com/exasol/saas-terraform/pull/1255) fixed the missing `bin/` path for `filter-datadog`; [exasol/saas-terraform#1256](https://github.com/exasol/saas-terraform/pull/1256) fixed the deeper issue where a missing S3 zip could be detected but not acted on when the local source hash was unchanged.
- Operational cleanup: briefly deleted the problematic staging hash marker and retriggered the release deployment during the investigation. Mihai later raised that two SaaS DB records were still in `error` with `last_modified_by: stepfunction` since Aug 6, while the SLI page appeared incomplete or misleading about deletion completion.
- Jira worklogs: no Aug 18 Jira worklogs were returned for the current user. The broad updated-issue search mostly surfaced older resolved L3 issues touched by later updates rather than clear Aug 18 hands-on work.
